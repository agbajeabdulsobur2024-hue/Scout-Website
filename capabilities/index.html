Zones
01

Demand & supply zones

Identified from market structure.

02

Historical zone discovery

Finds historically significant zones across all available price history.

03

Strongest / nearest / relevant

Three different rankings for the zones tracked on a symbol — relevant is not the same as strongest.

04

Zone lifecycle

CANDIDATE (untested) → OBSERVED (visited once) → TESTED (scored) → INVALIDATED (always overrides). Invalidated zones are excluded from both rankings.

Trend Intelligence
01

15M trend changes

A lower-timeframe (15M) trend change can be reported independently of higher-timeframe structure — e.g. "15M Trend Changed Bearish" while 4H remains Bullish. 15M tells you what's happening now; 4H/1H tells you the broader context.

02

HTF trend changes

When the higher timeframe itself changes, Scout communicates that separately from a 15M shift — the two are never conflated.

03

Lower-timeframe setups vs. HTF context

A valid 15M setup that conflicts with HTF bias isn't hidden. Scout surfaces the 15M setup, the HTF bias, the conflict, and relevant HTF structure together — framed as a lower-timeframe opportunity to manage risk around, not a guaranteed call.

Current-State Setup Analysis
01

Live re-evaluation

Asking for a setup reflects current price, current 15M structure, and 1H/4H context every time — Scout doesn't repeat an old thesis just because HTF bias hasn't changed.

02

Entry readiness

If a setup is no longer entry-ready, or price needs to return to a setup area first, Scout says so directly instead of restating stale analysis.

Scout Social & Insights
01

Scout Insights

Real Scout market intelligence — analysis, a monitored condition, a zone, a setup, an alert — turned into a structured, shareable Insight via /insight. Private by default; /share and /unshare control what becomes public.

02

Insight lifecycle

WATCHING → TRIGGERED → CONFIRMED → INVALIDATED / COMPLETED.

03

Public profiles

/profile [@handle] views a public profile — bio, followed markets, public Insight count and recent Insights, follower count. /setbio sets your own.

04

Following

/follow//unfollow a person, /followmarket//unfollowmarket a symbol, /following lists both.

05

Feeds & discovery

/feed (personalized), /marketfeed (one symbol), /discover (users to follow), /trending (hot symbols).

06

Market-level social intelligence

/market <symbol> aggregates only what users have made public — watcher counts, condition-type breakdown, most-watched zones, recently triggered Insights.

07

Reputation

Self-reported win/loss/breakeven via /outcome, shown as a transparent stat block on a user's own profile. Not audited by Scout, not a leaderboard, not comparable across users.

Access

Setup generation, zone queries, thesis, comparisons, and explanations are 1/day on Free, unlimited on Pro.
