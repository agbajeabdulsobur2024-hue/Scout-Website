What Scout
actually does

Scout scans the market, identifies structure, finds meaningful zones and setups, monitors the conditions a trader cares about, and alerts them when something changes — explaining why, not just that it happened. It runs on its own deterministic market-logic and intent-parsing engine, not a general-purpose AI model.

Why Scout, not ChatGPT or Claude

A different job, not a smarter answer

If you want to ask a question and get an answer, a general-purpose AI tool already does that well. Scout is built for ongoing market work: you tell it what matters, it monitors continuously, and you can come back later to exactly what you were watching — instead of starting over each time.

What makes Scout different

It separates diagnosis from prescription

i

Explains why

Not a signal-only bot. Every read comes with the structure, liquidity, and zone reasoning behind it.

ii

Remembers what you flagged

Scout stays on the conditions you told it matter, without you asking again.

iii

Pauses and resumes

A trader can pause a setup and pick it back up later, instead of starting over.

iv

Says "nothing yet"

A useful answer can be that nothing currently requires your attention — Scout won't manufacture urgency.

v

Diagnosis, not prescription

Scout tells you what's happening and why. You decide what to do about it.

vi

Deterministic engine

Runs on Scout's own market logic and intent-parsing — not a general-purpose AI model.
