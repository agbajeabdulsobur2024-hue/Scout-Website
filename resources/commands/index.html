Resources / Commands

Command reference

Every command Scout supports on Telegram. Most capabilities also work in plain language — this list is for when you want to be exact.

CommandWhat it does
/setupsRanked A/A+ opportunity list across majors and MEXC movers
/opportunitiesSame as /setups
/oppShorthand for /opportunities
/majorsSnapshot of major-asset market state
/timelineRecent history for a symbol
/strategySave, view, switch, or delete a custom strategy (Pro)
/chart SYMBOLChart image for a symbol
/watchlistSee your active simple watches
/workflowsSee your active trade-plan monitors
/clearwatchlistClear all watches at once
/historyPast monitoring history
/playbook SYMBOLTrack SMC playbook progress for a symbol
/journalAdd or view journal entries
/journalsReview all journal entries
/statsYour own journal statistics
/accuracyScout's own verdict accuracy report
/addwatch SYMBOLAdd a symbol to your personal watchlist
/removewatch SYMBOLRemove a symbol from your personal watchlist
/ignore SYMBOLExclude a symbol from your results
/unignore SYMBOLRe-include a previously ignored symbol
/minconfidenceSet your minimum confidence bar
/minrrSet your minimum risk/reward bar
/modeSwitch explanation depth: beginner or pro
/accountsizeSet your account size for position sizing
/riskSet your risk percentage per trade
/sizeCalculate position size
/preferencesSet communication style
/briefinghourSet your daily briefing time
/timezoneSet your timezone
/trackmoversConfigure MEXC mover tracking
/biasQuick directional bias read
/fundingFunding-rate information
/analysis_chatOpen-ended market analysis conversation
/upgradeStart Scout Pro checkout
/scanManual market scan (Pro)
/startStart using Scout
/insightTurn an active condition into a private, shareable Insight
/myinsights, /insightsList your own Insights, private and public
/sharePublish a private Insight — its public link starts resolving
/unshareMake a shared Insight private again
/setbioSet or clear your Scout Social bio
/profile [@handle]View a public Scout Social profile
/follow, /unfollowFollow/unfollow a person's public activity
/followmarket, /unfollowmarketFollow/unfollow a symbol's public activity
/followingList who and what you follow
/feedYour personalized feed from follows
/marketfeedOne symbol's public activity feed
/discoverSurface other users to follow
/trendingCurrently trending symbols on Scout Social
/market <symbol>A symbol's Scout Social summary — distinct from Scout's own market analysis
/outcomeRecord a win/loss/breakeven outcome (feeds journal stats and Social reputation)

A few commands — including /playbook and most account/admin commands — are slash-command-only by design.
