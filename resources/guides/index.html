How-To Guides

What to say, and exactly what you get back — organized by what you're trying to do.

Market

"how's ETH looking?"
Structure, zones, liquidity, and a plain TRADE / WAIT / IGNORE read, with reasoning.
"market map for SOL"
One composed view: current structure, the zones that matter right now, and why.
"what's the thesis on ETH?"
Current market thesis, built fresh from structure every time — not cached.
"what's different on BTC since yesterday?"
What changed, via /timeline BTC or natural language.
"/majors"
A quick structural read across the major assets at once.

Discovery

"/setups or /opportunities"
Ranked opportunities across majors and MEXC movers, personalized to your profile.
"show opportunities by category"
A category breakdown instead of one flat ranked list.
"compare BTC and ETH"
A side-by-side comparison under Scout's default analysis.
"compare BEEM and ICT on SOL"
Compares how two named strategy lenses read the same symbol.
"/briefinghour, /timezone"
Sets your daily briefing schedule. Free gets the core briefing; Pro adds Top Opportunities, What Changed, Overnight Movers, Today's Plan.
"/strategy"
Save, view, switch, or delete a personal saved strategy (Pro).
"/chart BTC"
A chart image for the symbol.

Zones

"historical zones on ETH"
Searches all available price history for significant zones.
"strongest zones on BTC / nearest zones / relevant zones"
Three different rankings — relevant is not the same as strongest.
"relevant demand zones on SOL"
Filters to a specific relevant-zone type.
"check the evidence on this zone"
Its lifecycle stage: CANDIDATE (untested), OBSERVED (visited once), TESTED (scored), or INVALIDATED (broken — always overrides).
"watch the demand zone on ETH"
Sets a monitor directly on a specific zone.

Monitoring

"watch BTC above 105,000"
A price-level watch.
"watch BTC between 100,000 and 105,000"
A range watch.
"watch AGI for rejection at 0.01024"
A rejection watch — price tests and fails a level.
"watch for a break of structure on SOL"
A structural-change watch.
"watch for a breakout entry on ETH above the range high, stop below 2400"
Becomes a full trade-plan monitor — entry, stop, and condition all tracked together.
"cancel that / cancel my BTC watch"
Stops a specific watch. /clearwatchlist clears everything.
"pause my BTC setup ... resume my BTC setup"
Pauses a setup, then picks it back up later with nothing re-described.
"/watchlist, /workflows"
See everything you're watching — simple watches and trade-plan monitors, respectively.
"/playbook BTC"
Tracks SMC playbook progress for a symbol (command-only by design).

Trading

"why WAIT on ETH?"
Explain Yourself Mode — shows exactly which conditions are met and unmet.
"what's missing for this setup?"
Names the specific missing condition.
"show me the risk/reward on this"
Entry, stop, and targets together — always distinguishes a confirmed structure target from a projected one.

Context

"why did you alert me on this?"
Traces the alert back to the exact condition and market context that triggered it.
"/journal BTC entered on FVG retest"
Logs a journal entry. Review with /journals, stats with /stats, accuracy with /accuracy.

Personalization

"/addwatch SOL, /removewatch SOL"
Adds or removes a symbol from your personal watchlist.
"/ignore SYMBOL, /unignore SYMBOL"
Excludes or re-includes a symbol from your results.
"/minconfidence, /minrr"
Sets your confidence and risk/reward bar.
"/mode"
Switches explanation depth between beginner and pro.
"/accountsize, /risk, /size"
Sets position sizing based on your account and risk tolerance.
"/preferences"
Sets communication style: concise, analytical, educational, professional, or balanced.
