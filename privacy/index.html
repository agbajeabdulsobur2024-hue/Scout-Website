Applies toUse of Scout on Telegram

1. Our Approach to Privacy

Scout is designed to minimize the personal information required to operate the Service.

Scout primarily operates through Telegram and ordinarily uses the user's Telegram identity as the core user identifier.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, Scout may process your Telegram identifier, Scout account identifier, preferences, watchlists, saved configurations, subscription information, payment records, payment-provider identifiers, payment status and events, timestamps, information you voluntarily provide, and technical information necessary to operate and secure the Service.

Scout does not require users to provide private cryptocurrency keys or seed phrases.

3. Why We Process Information

We may process information to provide and personalize Scout, maintain subscriptions, reconcile payments, provide support, prevent duplicate or fraudulent transactions, maintain security, diagnose technical issues, improve the Service, and comply with applicable legal obligations where required.

4. Payment Information

Scout may receive payment-status and transaction-related information from NOWPayments or related payment infrastructure so that Scout can determine whether a payment corresponds to a Scout user and whether Paid Service access should be activated.

Scout does not need your private key or seed phrase to process a normal subscription payment.

5. Third-Party Providers

Scout may use third-party providers for Telegram communication, market data, cloud hosting, databases, payment processing, security, monitoring, and other infrastructure.

Those providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Security

Scout takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information under its control.

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

You should protect your Telegram account and never disclose private keys, seed phrases, or passwords.

7. Retention

Scout retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for service operation, security, payment reconciliation, dispute resolution, accounting, fraud prevention, and applicable legal obligations.

Different categories of records may therefore be retained for different periods.

8. Your Privacy Requests

Where applicable and subject to legal and technical limitations, you may contact Scout regarding information held about you, correction of inaccurate information, deletion requests, or other privacy concerns.

Scout may require reasonable verification before processing an account-specific request.

9. Privacy Contact

All privacy and data-protection questions or requests should be sent to hq.scout@gmail.com.
