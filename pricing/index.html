FAQ

Common questions

Is Foundational a different plan from Pro?

No. Foundational Membership is a founding-member price on the exact same Scout Pro tier — $20/month while the initial quota lasts, instead of the standard $30/month. Once the quota fills, new members pay $30/month for identical features.

What stays free, always?

Core market understanding ($SYMBOL analysis, quick verdicts, bias, funding, Market Map, playbook), trust and education (accuracy report, analysis chat, FAQ), and core ownership (journal, basic charts, basic watchlist) are deliberately never paywalled.

How do I pay?

USDT on Ethereum, BSC, Arbitrum, or Tron, via NOWPayments. Base is not currently supported.

How do I upgrade?

Send /upgrade to Scout on Telegram to start checkout.

Does Scout guarantee results?

No. Scout is a decision-support tool, not financial or investment advice, and does not guarantee trading outcomes. See the Trading & Risk Disclaimer.
