Scout Social

Private intelligence
when you need it.
Shared when you choose.

Scout Social turns real Scout market intelligence into shareable, discoverable market insights. It isn't a generic feed — every Insight is backed by structured Scout intelligence, not a free-text post.

The core object

An Insight, not a post

An Insight can originate from Scout activity — market analysis, a monitored condition, a zone, a setup, a market change, an alert, or a triggered monitoring event. What makes it an Insight rather than a caption is that it's backed by the same structured intelligence Scout uses privately.

How it works
01

Scout detects something useful

A structural change, a triggered condition, a completed analysis — the same intelligence Scout already generates for you privately.

02

You choose to share it

Nothing becomes public automatically. You decide which piece of intelligence is worth publishing as an Insight.

03

Scout produces a shareable Insight

A structured, public representation of that intelligence — not a screenshot, not a paraphrase.

04

Someone else discovers it

They see real Scout intelligence in action, and can find their way into Scout themselves.

Privacy

Private by default. Public only by your choice.

Scout monitoring stays private unless you explicitly share it through the Insight mechanism. Nothing below is ever exposed on its own:

—

Never exposed automatically

Private watchlists, private workflows, account information, risk settings, private notes, personal preferences, and private monitoring conditions all stay private unless you explicitly share a specific Insight.

Following

Follow a trader, or a market

/follow a person's public activity, or /followmarket a symbol's. /feed shows your personalized feed from both; /discover and /trending help you find who and what to follow next.

CommandDoes
/follow, /unfollowFollow or unfollow a person's public activity
/followmarket, /unfollowmarketFollow or unfollow a symbol's public activity
/followingList everyone and every market you follow
/feedYour personalized feed from your follows
/marketfeedOne symbol's public activity feed on its own
/discoverSurfaces other users to follow
/trendingSymbols currently seeing the most public Insight activity
/market <symbol>What the Scout network is watching on that symbol — watcher counts, condition-type breakdown, most-watched zones
Reputation

Self-reported. Never a leaderboard.

Record a real win/loss/breakeven outcome on the condition an Insight came from with /outcome. Your own /profile then shows a transparent stat block: resolved count and win rate. This is explicitly self-reported and not audited by Scout — not a leaderboard, not a cross-user comparison, not comparable across different sample sizes. The methodology is always shown alongside the numbers.

Available now vs. future direction

What's live, and what's next

Available now

Sharing an Insight, public profiles with bio and history, following people and markets, a personalized feed, per-market feed, discovery and trending feeds, market-level social intelligence per symbol, and self-reported, unaudited reputation.

Future direction

Likes/comments or any engagement-metric ranking — deliberately excluded by design, not just unbuilt. Cross-user leaderboards or profitability comparisons — explicitly out of scope. Topic/category following beyond people and markets — the data model supports it, but it isn't live.
