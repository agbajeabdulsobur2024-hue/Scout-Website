Applies toUse of Scout on Telegram

1. Payment Provider

Scout uses NOWPayments as a third-party cryptocurrency payment processor.

Payment checkout may be initiated through Scout's Telegram experience where the applicable implementation supports it.

2. Payment Instructions

You must pay the exact cryptocurrency and network displayed at checkout.

Current supported USDT networks are Ethereum (ERC-20), BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20), Arbitrum, and Tron (TRC-20). Base is currently unsupported.

Scout is not responsible for losses caused by sending an unsupported asset, using an unsupported network, or sending funds to an incorrect destination.

3. Payment Activation

Scout uses payment-provider status information and its internal payment records to reconcile a payment with the Scout user who initiated checkout.

Access may remain pending until the payment reaches the status required for activation.

4. Failed, Duplicate, or Unmatched Payments

If a payment is duplicated, incorrectly processed, unmatched, or appears successful externally but does not activate the relevant service, contact hq.scout@gmail.com.

Scout may request transaction information or other reasonable information necessary to investigate the issue.

5. Cancellation

You may request cancellation through the available Scout support process.

Where recurring billing is offered, a successful cancellation prevents future renewal.

Cancellation does not automatically create a refund for a subscription period that has already started.

6. Refunds

Refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis.

Examples that may justify a refund include duplicate payment, verified payment-processing error, payment made but Paid Service not activated because of a Scout-side technical failure, or another verified circumstance that Scout determines warrants a refund.

Simply deciding not to use the Service after successful activation does not automatically create a refund entitlement.

7. Incorrect Cryptocurrency Payments

Blockchain transactions are generally irreversible.

If you send funds through an unsupported network, use the wrong asset, or send to an incorrect destination, Scout will make reasonable efforts to investigate but cannot guarantee recovery.

8. Approved Refunds

Where a refund is approved, Scout will determine the appropriate method based on the original payment and the technical capabilities of the payment provider.

Refund timing may depend on third-party processing and blockchain conditions.

9. Contact

Payment, refund, and cancellation requests should be sent to hq.scout@gmail.com.
